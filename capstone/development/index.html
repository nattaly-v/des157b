Capstone Proposal

Topic

My capstone project's main focus is immigration support/resources and how to digitalize tools to make information more accessible and less overwhelming for immigrants in the US. I want to explore and find a way to design something that can help my target audiences better understand the current immigration systems, their rights, and the resources available for them. My vision for the projects is to design either an interactive website or an application that is clear, accessible, and provides support.

Lenses

My project's main lenses are social justice, speculative design, challenging mental models, and visual data. I am here to challenge the idea that people with little knowledge of immigration have that the systems are easy to navigate and that information is equally accessible to everyone. I am also interested in imagining how the future can have digital systems that support people rather than exploit them. Cybersecurity being a concern for these groups makes me want to create something secure and easy to use. I might add visual data to provide immigration timelines, processing times, and resource research.

Features, Content, and Audience

With this topic being important to me, I’ve previously thought about features for a project like this. Some possible features could include a “Know Your Rights” section, step-by-step guides, language accessibility options, resource maps, and interactive timelines based on the data and research I collect. JavaScript libraries may be useful for animations and data visualization throughout the experience. One challenge will be finding a way to make the information feel understandable and accessible without overwhelming users the way many legal websites do. I need to consider that my audience is looking for clarity and support, not information overload. My target audience will focus specifically on undocumented Latino and Spanish-speaking immigrants in the United States, especially older adults with limited technology knowledge who may struggle with navigating complex systems and legal information.
