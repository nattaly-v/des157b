Source 1

Migration Policy Institute. “Features of U.S. State and Local Language Access Laws and Policies.” Migration Policy Institute ,www.migrationpolicy.org/events/features-us-state-local-language-access-laws-policies.

This source focuses on language access laws and policies in the United States, especially for Limited English Proficient (LEP) individuals. The Migration Policy Institute states that there are laws in place for people to have the right to language access. It goes over the importance og providing accessible info and services to non-English speakers. This research is really important for immigrants to have easy access to information and services in their native language in order to stay informed and educated. The Migration Policy Institute is a credible research organization focused on migration and immigration policy. From this source, I concluded that language accessibility is an important part of equity and that my project should focus on making information simpler, multilingual, and easier to understand.

Source 2

Lopez, Mark Hugo, et al. “Around Four-in-Ten Latinos in U.S. Worry That They or Someone Close to Them Could Be Deported.” Pew Research Center, 14 Feb. 2022, http://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2022/02/14/around-four-in-ten-latinos-in-u-s-worry-that-they-or-someone-close-to-them-could-be-deported/.

This article from the Pew Research Center discusses the fear and anxiety many Latinos and immigrants in the United States experience regarding deportation. This source provides stats of the great concerns Latino immigrants have about immigration, specifically undocumented immigrants and people without a green card. A lot of immigrants may not know where to turn for help and are afraid of getting deported. This source is important for my research because it will help me better understand how emotionally straining this process is for immigrants who struggle with the system. The Pew Research Center is a credible source because of its accurate data collection. I concluded that my project should not only provide information, but should also feel emotionally supportive, safe, and accessible for users during stressful situations.
